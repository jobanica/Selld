# What Makes This Defensible

## What Shopify can't and won't build

Not "hasn't yet" — **won't**, because the PH market isn't worth the product complexity to them.

- **COD as a first-class citizen** — with RTS tracking and remittance reconciliation
- **J&T / Flash / LBC / Ninja native bulk booking**
- **Facebook comment-to-order live selling capture**
- **Taglish checkout and SMS**
- **₱1,499 price point with white-glove onboarding**

That last line is the real barrier. A ₱1,499 price point with human onboarding only works
if your cost structure and geography make it work. Shopify's don't.

## What local competitors don't have

- **Existing distribution** — Servd audience, founder brand, content engine
- **A shipped, proven product as social proof** — 9 named Davao restaurants already running
- **Build speed** — with Claude Code

---

## The wedge

Same as Servd:

> **Hyper-local operational reality + flat fee.**

Don't compete on features. Compete on:

> **"Gawa ito para sa'yo, at hindi ka kinakaltasan."**

Feature competition is a race Shopify wins on resources and local competitors win on
copying speed. Operational reality plus a flat fee is a position, and positions are
defensible in a way that feature lists aren't.
