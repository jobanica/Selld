# The Avatar

We build for one person, not a segment. Her name is Rhea.

## Who she is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name / age / location** | Rhea, 31 — Davao / Cebu / Cavite |
| **Business** | "Rhea's Finds," 2 years old. Skincare + RTW. |
| **Channels** | Facebook page (28k followers), IG (6k), TikTok Shop, Shopee |
| **Team** | Herself + 1 sister packing + 1 part-time encoder |
| **Monthly revenue** | ₱280,000 |
| **Monthly orders** | ~420 |
| **Net margin she *thinks* she has** | 30% |
| **Net margin she *actually* has** | 14% |

That last pair is the whole business case. The gap between 30% and 14% is what we sell against.

## A day in her life

- **6am** — Check Messenger. 63 unread. 18 are "how much po," 22 are "nasaan na po order ko," 9 are "cancel na lang po."
- **9am** — Encode yesterday's orders into Excel from screenshots of DMs.
- **11am** — Realize she sold the same last piece twice — sa FB at sa Shopee. Has to cancel one. Shopee dings her rating.
- **2pm** — Live selling. 200+ comments of "MINE po." Sister writes names on paper. Three get missed.
- **5pm** — Book 40 parcels one by one sa J&T portal. Copy-paste every address. 90 minutes.
- **8pm** — Reply "on the way na po" to 30 people manually.
- **11pm** — Opens Shopee payout. ₱46,000 gross → ₱41,200 after commission and fees. Sighs.

She doesn't know if she made money this month. Genuinely doesn't know.

## What she has already tried

Every one of these is a failed solution we must be visibly different from.

| Tried | Why it failed |
|---|---|
| Shopify | ₱1,600+/mo, USD billing, walang COD, no J&T, English-only checkout, apps cost more than the plan |
| Free website builders | No inventory, no COD, no courier, looked cheap, walang bumili |
| Google Sheets + forms | Nag-work sa 50 orders/mo. Nag-collapse sa 400. |
| Hiring an encoder | Just moved the chaos to another person. Still errors. |
| "Iiwan na lang ang FB, focus sa Shopee" | Commission went up. Forced promos. Ratings ruled her life. |

## What she actually says

Use these **verbatim** in ads. Do not clean them up into English.

- "Ang laki ng bawas sa Shopee."
- "Nakakapagod na yung manual encoding."
- "Sana may isang lugar lang lahat ng orders."
- "Gusto ko sarili kong website pero mahal at complicated."
- "Nawawala ang customer list ko kapag nag-down ang page ko."

## Her three real fears

1. **Platform risk** — one FB page restriction and her business is gone overnight.
2. **Invisible bleed** — she suspects the fees are eating her but can't prove it.
3. **Being stuck** — she can't grow past ~500 orders/mo because she *is* the system.

## Disqualifiers — do NOT sell to

Selling to these people costs more in churn and support than they pay us.

- **Under ~40 orders/month** — manual pa rin ang tama, mag-churn siya
- **Pure dropshippers with no brand** — no repeat-buyer value, price-only buyers
- **Sellers with zero social following** — Selld distributes orders, it doesn't create demand
- **Pure marketplace sellers na walang FB/IG presence** — nothing for us to sit on top of
