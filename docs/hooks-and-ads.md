# Hooks & Ad Angles

## Primary emotional lever

**Commission pain.** Same as Servd — pero mas raw ang nerve dito kasi *monthly* nilang
nakikita ang bawas sa payout statement. The wound reopens every payout cycle. We don't
have to create the pain; we just have to name it.

---

## Hook bank — Taglish, cold audience

1. "Bawat ₱1,000 na sale mo sa Shopee, ₱80 ang bawas. Sa isang taon, ₱96,000 na 'yon. Ilang buwan mo ba binayaran ang commission na 'yan?"
2. "Ang customer list mo — hindi sa'yo 'yan. Pag-aari 'yan ng platform."
3. "Kung nag-down ang Facebook page mo ngayon, may business ka pa ba bukas?"
4. "40 parcels, one by one sa J&T portal. 90 minutes. Araw-araw. May mas madali pa."
5. "200 comments na 'MINE po'. Ilan kaya ang nakalimutan mo?"
6. "Hindi mo alam kung kumita ka ba this month, 'no?"
7. "Isang bayad kada buwan. Zero commission. Kahit ₱1M ang sales mo."
8. "Ganito ka na dalawang taon. Ilan pang order bago ka mag-system?"

Each of these maps to something in [`avatar.md`](avatar.md) — her verbatim quotes, her
day, or her three fears. New hooks must do the same.

## Content pillars

Mirror ng Servd's four.

1. **Commission Escape** — fee math, payout teardowns, "ilan ang binayaran mo" calculators
2. **Operations** — packing flow, courier hacks, inventory sanity, RTS reduction
3. **Sell More** — live selling tactics, repeat buyer plays, product page copy
4. **Build in Public** — same as Servd. Founder face, real numbers, real client wins.

---

## Lead magnet — Commission Bleed Calculator

She inputs monthly sales + which platforms she's on. It outputs her **annual fee bleed**
and the Selld comparison. Emails/SMS her the result.

This is the single highest-intent capture we can build, and it doubles as ad creative:
the calculator output *is* the math slide, personalized. Anyone who completes it has
self-identified revenue, channel mix, and pain — the exact three fields the qualifying
flow otherwise has to ask for.

---

## Messenger qualifying flow

**Keyword: `STORE`**

1. **"Saan ka nagbebenta ngayon?"** → FB / IG / TikTok Shop / Shopee-Lazada / Lahat
2. **"Ilang orders kada buwan?"** → <50 / 50-200 / 200-500 / 500+
3. **"Ano pinakasakit sa'yo?"** → Commission / Manual encoding / Shipping / Inventory / Walang website
4. **"May live selling ka ba?"** → Oo, regular / Sometimes / Wala

**Routing:**

- **Qualified** (200+ orders, commission or encoding pain) → book demo
- **Unqualified** (<50 orders) → Starter free tier signup, nurture sequence

Note that question 4 isn't a qualifier — it's a **tier router**. "Oo, regular" means Pro,
because live selling capture is the Pro feature she'll switch platforms for.
