# Go-To-Market Sequence

## Weeks 1–2 — Proof before scale

Onboard **3 sellers free** — ideally in Davao, ideally with live selling.

Document everything: before/after time spent, orders processed, commission avoided.
**This is your entire ad library.** No paid spend happens before this exists, because
without it we're making claims instead of showing receipts — and this avatar has already
been sold to by people making claims.

## Weeks 3–4 — Founding Seller cohort

Open **30 slots** at locked pricing. Sell via:

- DM outreach to FB seller groups
- Your existing Servd audience — some restaurant clients also sell products, so this is a
  warm cross-sell, not cold outreach

30 is the real white-glove onboarding ceiling, which is what makes the scarcity honest.

## Month 2 — Paid

Facebook/IG ads → **Commission Bleed Calculator**. Retarget calculator completers with the
Grand Slam Offer + guarantee.

Use existing Meta learning-phase knowledge from Servd. Same ad account maturity, same
audience geography, same creative instincts — this is a real head start over a cold start.

## Month 3 — Live selling wedge

**This is the moat.** Go hard on comment-to-order as the headline feature.

Nobody in PH does this well, and it's the feature sellers will switch platforms for.
Everything before this month is about earning the right to be believed when we make this
claim.

## Month 4+ — Reseller / white-label

Same three-tier structure explored for the Servd platform:

> you → agency/reseller → seller

Barangay-level and city-level resellers in PH is a real distribution channel, not a
theoretical one. The Scale tier's white-label feature set exists to serve this.

---

## The sequencing logic

Proof → warm distribution → paid → differentiation → leverage.

Each phase funds and de-risks the next. Skipping to paid before proof is the single most
expensive mistake available here.
