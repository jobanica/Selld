# The Value Equation

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

Four levers. Two we push up, two we drive toward zero.

---

## Dream Outcome — push up

> "Every order, from every channel, in one dashboard — and I keep 100% of every peso."

Not "a website." The dream is that **she stops being the system**, and she stops paying
rent on her own sales.

The concrete version we actually sell:

- 400 orders/month handled by 1 person in **45 minutes/day**
- **₱0 commission**
- Her own customer list, hers forever
- She can answer **"kumita ba ako?"** in 5 seconds

## Perceived Likelihood — raise it

She has been burned by Shopify, by builders, by spreadsheets. Belief is the bottleneck,
not desire.

- **9 named Davao restaurants already running on Servd** — same builder, proven track record
- **Public case study** — real seller, real before/after numbers, screenshots
- **"Import your existing product list — we do it for you"**
- **Live 15-minute demo using *her own* products**
- **Month-to-month, no lock-in contract**

## Time Delay — crush it

- **Store live in 24 hours**, not 24 days
- **Done-for-you setup** — we upload her products, set her shipping rates, connect her courier
- **First 20 products encoded by us, free**
- She sees her **first real order through Selld on day 1**

## Effort & Sacrifice — remove it

- **Migration done for her** — products, customers, price list
- **No coding, no domain hassle** — subdomain instant, custom domain optional
- **Runs on her phone**
- **Taglish interface** option
- **We don't ask her to abandon Shopee/FB** — Selld sits *on top* of what she already has

That last point is the one most competitors get wrong. Asking her to leave the channels
where her demand already lives is asking her to bet the business. We never ask that.
